# Mission: Product Thinking (via IDG Singapore)

## Why
Move into a product manager role (public or private sector — still exploring). Product thinking is the foundation; the Institute of Digital Governance's material is the grounding lens.

## Success looks like
- Explain core product-thinking concepts fluently, in my own words, in a PM interview
- Answer common PM interview prompts (e.g. "how would you improve X?") using a clear problem-first structure
- Produce one portfolio case study: problem → discovery → solution → measuring outcomes
- Talk through that case study confidently, including trade-offs and what I'd do differently

## Constraints
- ~10 minutes per session — lessons must be bite-sized
- Starting from zero: brand new to deliberate product practice

## Out of scope (for now)
- Deep technical skills (engineering, data science tooling)
- Specific PM tool mastery (Jira, Figma, etc.)
