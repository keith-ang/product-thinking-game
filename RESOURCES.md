# Product Thinking Resources

## Knowledge

- [IDG: Product Thinking (landing)](https://www.idg.gov.sg/product-thinking/)
  Institute of Digital Government (MDDI) 7-module course; the "Frame" step of AI Build 301. Use for: the core spine of this curriculum.
- [IDG M1: Understanding the Problem](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking1/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DqGP6BvyRdk)
  The Common Trap, three principles, "nail one problem first". Use for: mindset, problems vs solutions, outcomes vs outputs.
- [IDG M2: Start With The Whys](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking2/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdzcM6ax5YY)
  Five Whys, choosing the right level to fix. Use for: root cause analysis.
- [IDG M3: Craft a Clear Problem Statement](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking3/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBSAouM7iaE)
  4Cs (Clarity, Consequence, Cause, Confirmation) with a worked example. Use for: problem statements, portfolio case study framing.
- [IDG M4: Metrics](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking4/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rtb_tlSzTJ0)
  SMART, leading/lagging indicators, Value-Cost Ratio. Use for: measuring outcomes.
- [IDG M5: Assumptions and Risks](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking5/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_iZNhzdLd7A)
  Market/technical/team risk, four stages of de-risking, SG health booking A/B test. Use for: staged delivery, experiments.
- [IDG M6: A Good Customer Experience](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking6/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMe7WgWzIe8)
  Design principles, ParkingSG / Redeem.sg, 11-star framework. Use for: CX, trade-offs.
- [IDG M7: Key Takeaways](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking7/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sI5veUTIzkk)
  Three shifts, the toolkit, "now apply it". Use for: review and spaced retrieval.
- [IDG Capstone (AI 301)](https://www.idg.gov.sg/build-with-ai-capstone/)
  Worked 4Cs example (shift rostering). Use for: case-study template.
- [OGP: Product Management role](https://www.open.gov.sg/careers/what-we-do/product-management/)
  What a public-sector PM actually does at Open Government Products. Use for: career/role expectations.
- [GovTech Digital Service Standards — Understanding Users](https://info.standards.tech.gov.sg/control-catalog/dss/uu/)
  UU-1 understand user needs, UU-2 test with users. Use for: user research (IDG doesn't cover it).
- [GovTech: 3 Takeaways on PM in the Singapore Government](https://medium.com/dsaid-govtech/3-takeaways-on-product-management-in-the-singapore-government-from-govtechs-first-pm-0a91a59e682c)
  Public-sector PM nuance ("cater for 100% of Singapore"). Use for: public vs private PM differences.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [ProductTank Singapore (Mind the Product)](https://www.meetup.com/producttank-singapore/)
  Free regular SG meetups (~50–70 people). Use for: meeting working PMs, informational chats, testing your case study.
- [Mind the Product](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/)
  Largest global product community; articles and conferences. Use for: broader industry perspective.
- [GOV.UK Product and service community](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/communities)
  Public-sector PM practice; reading is public. Use for: how government PM is done elsewhere.

## Gaps

- IDG does not cover: product vs project, MVP terminology, discovery vs delivery, product roles, user research methods, PM interview practice. Need high-trust sources for these (e.g. Marty Cagan / SVPG, Teresa Torres, GOV.UK Service Manual).
- CRAFT by GovTech and the LEARN course "Build What Matters" are public-officer-only (403 / login) — not accessible.
