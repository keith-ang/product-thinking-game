Product Thinking Toolkit

A one-page summary of IDG’s product thinking. It grows with each lesson.

Definition

Product thinking: “a discipline of asking better questions before reaching for solutions.” [IDG M7]

Principle 1 · Problems before solutions

Covered in Lesson 1

The three questions
  1. Who are our users?
  2. What are they trying to do?
  3. What’s getting in their way?

Solution in disguise: a request that names a thing to build instead of a user’s obstacle.
Test: if it can only be fixed one way, it’s a solution.

Reframe template: “[User] wants to [goal], but [obstacle].”

Sounds likeReal issue (IDG examples)
“We need more AI.”Officers struggle to decide from unwieldy datasets.
“We need a superapp.”Citizens can’t access services efficiently across platforms.
“We don’t have a CRM.”Cumbersome processes → long citizen wait times.
“Our product is end of life.”Citizens and officers will lose access to critical services.

Interview move: when asked to “design X” or “improve Y”, ask the three questions before naming any features.
